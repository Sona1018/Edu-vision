Question 1: Define Marketing. Explain the nature of marketing along with examples.
REFERENCE ANSWER: Marketing is the social and managerial process of identifying customer needs, creating products or services, communicating their value, and delivering satisfaction to customers while achieving organizational goals. It is customer-oriented, a continuous and dynamic process, focused on value creation and exchange, and involves integrated marketing mix activities (4Ps).
MARKING SCHEME:
- Definition of Marketing (2 Marks): Concept of identifying needs and delivering value
- Customer-Centric Nature (2 Marks): Fulfilling customer needs rather than merely selling
- Continuous Process (2 Marks): Evolving with trends, technology, and preferences
- Value Creation and Exchange (2 Marks): Voluntary exchange where both parties gain value
- Integrated 4P Activities & Examples (2 Marks): Product, price, place, promotion with real-world examples

Question 2: What all are scope of marketing and importance of marketing?
REFERENCE ANSWER: The scope of marketing covers goods, services, experiences, events, persons, places, properties, organizations, information, and ideas. Its importance lies in revenue generation, brand equity, customer satisfaction, economic growth, and market expansion.
MARKING SCHEME:
- Scope Coverage (5 Marks): Goods, services, ideas, experiences, places, and personal branding
- Importance to Business & Economy (5 Marks): Customer relationship, revenue, and competitive advantage
