Q1 Define marketing & explain its nature. also
discuss how marketing helps in understanding
customer needs, creating value, delivering
value, identifying customer problems, and
ensuring customer satisfaction. Describe the
overall process of marketing ?

Ans Definition of marketing :-
According to Philip Kotler :-
Marketing is a social and managerial process
by which individuals & groups obtain what they
need and want through creating, offering &
exchanging products of value with others.

Marketing = Customer need + product creation 
            + value satisfaction + Delivery.

• Meaning of marketing :-
  Marketing is not only selling or advertising 
  it is a complete business philosophy that 
  starts with the customer & ends with 
  customer satisfaction.

  Marketing includes :-
• Studying customer needs.
• Designing suitable products.
• Pricing properly




• Promoting effectively
• Delivering at the right place & time

* Nature (characteristics) of marketing :-

① Customer oriented :- Marketing focuses on fulfilling
customer needs rather than just selling
products.

② Continuous process :- Marketing is ongoing ,
not a one-time activity

③ Value centered :- It emphasizes creating value
for customers.

④ Integrated activity :- Includes research, production
, pricing, promotion & distribution.

⑤ Exchange based :- Involves exchange of goods /
service for money or value

⑥ Goal-oriented :- Aims at customer satisfaction
& organizational profit.

⑦ Dynamic :- Marketing changes according to
trends, technology & customer behaviour.

Q.3 Differentiate between needs and wants ? Explain
how marketing influences the both ?

Ans Needs (जरूरते) :-
Needs are the basic necessities of life without
which human survival is not possible, these needs
are common for all human beings & do not change
frequently



Example:-
Food water , clothing , shelter, healthcare, education.

• WANTS (इच्छाएं) :-
Wants are the desire for specific goods or service
that satisfy needs in a particular way. Wants are
unlimited & influenced by culture, society, lifestyle and
marketing.

Example of wants :-
Pizza instead of simple food , branded clothes
instead of normal clothes , iPhone instead of basic
phone.

# How marketing influences both :-

Marketing mainly influences WANTS, but it also need
shapes how people satisfy their NEEDS.

① Through advertisement :-
Advertisements creates emotional appeal by showing
happiness, success, beauty & confidence linked
to products.
Ex :- A toothpaste ad shows white teeth = confidence
⇒ people feel they need that brand.

② Through branding :-
People prefer branded products even when cheaper
options are available.
Ex :- People want adidas shoes instead of local
shoes.



③ Through powerful celebrity endorsements :-
Celebrities promote products which increase desire
Ex :- A cricketer promoting a soft drink increases
its demand.

④ Through innovation & new product introduction :-
Marketing introduces new products that create
new wants.
Ex :- Before smartphones, people only needed calling
phones, now they want phones with camera, internet
, gaming etc

⑤ By converting need into wants :-
Marketing converting basic needs into specific wants.
Ex :- Need -> Clothing
Want -> Branded jeans, designer dresses.

⑥ Through social media & influencers :-
Trends shown online influence youth buying
behavior.
Ex :- Skincare routines shown by influencers create
demand for many products.

Q.4 Explain marketing environment ? Marketing environ-
ment characteristics & its types ?

Ans. Marketing environment :-
It refers to all internal & external forces,
factors and institutions that influence a
company's ability to develop & maintain successful
relationships with customers.




In simple words, it includes everything inside &
outside the organization that affects marketing
decisions.

# Characteristics of Marketing environment :-

① Dynamic (Ever changing) :-
Marketing environment continuously changes due to
technology, customer tastes, competition, &
government policies.
• Example :- Shift from offline shopping to online.

② Complex :-
It consists of many interrelated forces such as
social, economic, political & technological factors.
• Example :- A change in government tax policy affects
product's price & demand.

③ Uncontrollable :-
Many environmental factors cannot be controlled
by the firm.
Example :- Inflation or recession.

④ Inter-related :-
Change in one factor affects other factors.
Example :- Technological advancement affects consumer
lifestyle & buying behaviour

⑤ Affects business decisions :-
Marketing strategies depend heavily on environmental
conditions.




Example :- Eco-friendly trend -> companies introduce
bio-degradable packaging

# Types of marketing environment :-

① Internal environment :-
Factors inside the organization that can be
controlled & managed.

• Components of internal environment :-

(i) Top management :- Vision, mission, and policies of
top managers influence marketing.

(ii) Employees :- Skills, attitude and motivation of employees
affect service quality.

(iii) Organizational structure :- Department and coordination
affect efficiency.

(iv) Company culture :- Values & beliefs of the organi-
zation.

(v) Financial Resources :- Availability of funds for
advertising & promotion.

• For example :-
A company decides to increase its advertising budget
& train its marketing team to promote a new
product. This decision, along with its financial
resources & employee skills , is part of the
internal environment because these factors are



inside the company & can be controlled.

• External marketing environment :-
External marketing includes factors outside the
organization that affect business.

• It is divided into two types :-

| Micro environment | Macro environment |
| --- | --- |
| (1) Customers | (1) Economic factors |
| (2) Suppliers | (2) Social & cultural factors |
| (3) Competitors | (3) Technological factors |
| (4) Intermediaries | (4) Political & legal factors |
| (5) Publics | (5) Demographic factors |

• Example of External environment :-
If the government increases taxes and competitors
lower their prices, the company's sales may be
affected. These factors are part of the external
environment because they come from outside the
company & cannot be controlled.

Q.5 Explain micro & macro external environmental
factors ?

Ans MICRO external environment :-
Micro external environment can also be called
task environment.





• Micro external environment consists of factors
that are close the company & directly affect
its ability to serve customers.

① Customers :-
Customers are the most important part of
micro environment. They buy the product and
generate revenue.
• Types of customers :-
(i) Individual "
(ii) Business "
(iii) Government buyers.

Example :-
If customers prefer eco-friendly products,
companies must change packaging.

② Suppliers :-
Suppliers provide raw materials, components and
services.
• Importance :-
(i) Quality of raw material affects product quality.
(ii) Delay increases cost.

Example :-
If a supplier raises prices, the company may
increase product price.

③ Competitors :-
Firms offering similar or substitute products.
• Importance :-




(i) Influences pricing
(ii) Influences promotional strategies.

• Example :-
Pepsi & Coca-Cola competing through ads.

④ Marketing Intermediaries :-
Helps in promoting, selling and distributing
products.
• Types :-
• Wholesalers
• Retailers
• Transporters
• Advertising agencies.
• Example :- Amazon acting as an online retailer.

⑤ Publics :-
Groups that have interest in the company
• Types of publics :-
⇒ Media
⇒ Financial institutions
⇒ Government
⇒ Local community
• Example :- Negative media news can damage brand
image

② MACRO external environment :-

Macro environment consists of large forces
that affect all firms in an industry.




(1) Demographic factors :-
Population size, age, gender, income, education.
• Example :-
More youth population increases demand for
fashion products.

(2) Economic factors :-
Income level, inflation, interest rates, unemployment.
• Example :-
During recession people buy low-cost products.

(3) Social & cultural factors :-
Lifestyle, customs, traditions, values.
• Example :-
Health-conscious lifestyle increases demand for
organic food.

(4) Technological factors :-
New inventions & innovation.
• Example :-
Digital platform payments & online shopping methods.

(5) Political & legal factors :-
Government policies, laws, regulations.
• Example :-
Ban of plastic bags.

(6) Natural / environmental factors :-
Climate change, pollution, natural resources.
• Example :-
Use of biodegradable packaging.



Summary :-

• Micro environment = Customers + Suppliers +
Competitors + Intermediaries

* Publics factors

• Macro environment = Demographic + Economic +
Social + Technological + Political

* Natural factors.
