Q3. Explain Marketing Enviornment.
-> Marketing Enviornment refers to all internal and external
factors that affect a company's ability to serve its customer
successfully.
These factors influence marketing decisions, strategies and
performance.
The marketing enviornment is divided into ->

1. Internal
2. External < Macro
Micro



1. Internal Environment
Internal environment includes factors within the company
that affect marketing activities. These factors are generally
controllable.
Main Internal Factors ->
(1) Company Objectives
Marketing strategies depend on company goals
Eg. Tesla focus on sustainability, so its marketing highlights
clean energy.
(2) Management & Employees
Skilled employees and effective management improve
marketing success.
(3) Financial Resources
Budget determines advertising, promotion, expansion
capacity.
(4) Company Culture
Innovative culture leads to creative marketing strategies.
Eg- Apple promotes innovation and premium branding.
2. External Environment
External environment includes factors outside the company
that influence marketing. These are generally uncontrollable.
It is divided into ->
(1) Macro environment.
(2) Micro Environment.

Q5. Define Marketing. Explain nature of marketing.
-> Marketing is the process of identifying, anticipating and
satisfying customer needs and wants profitably.
According to AMA, Marketing is the activity, set of instructions
and processes for creating, communicating, delivering and
exchanging offers that have value for customers, clients,
partners and society at large. Marketing is not just
selling, it includes researching customer needs, designing
products, pricing them, promoting them, and distributing them
effectively.



Nature of Marketing

1. Customer Oriented -> It focuses on understanding
customers needs and satisfying them better than competitors.
Customer satisfaction is the main goal.
2. Goal-oriented -> It aims to achieve organisational goals
such as profit, growth & market share.
3. Exchange Process -> It involves exchange of goods/services
for money or value. Both buyer & seller should benefit
from the transaction.
4. Integrated Process -> It integrates various activities
like product design, pricing, promotion & distribution.
All departments work together to satisfy customers.
5. Continous Process -> Marketing is ongoing. Companies
continously research, improve, & adapt to market changes.
6. Creates utility -> It creates different utility.
• Form utility -> changing raw materials into finished
goods.
• Place utility -> Making goods available at right
place.
• Time utility -> Making goods available at right
time.
• Possession utility -> Transferring ownership to buyer
