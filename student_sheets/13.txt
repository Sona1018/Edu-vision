Q1 Define marketing. Explain nature of marketing.
According to American Marketing Association (AMA), marketing is
the activity, set of institutions, and processes for creating,
communicating, delivering, and exchanging offerings that have
value for customers, clients, partners and society at large.
In simple terms, marketing is the process of identifying
customer's needs and satisfying them profitably through
value creation and exchange.

Nature of marketing:

1. Customer centric : marketing starts and ends with the customer.
All business activities are directed towards understanding and
satisfying customer needs.
2. Exchange process : marketing is based on exchange - goods/
services are exchanged for money or other value. Both
parties must benefit from the exchange
3. Value oriented : modern marketing focuses on creating
superior value compared to competitors
4. Goal oriented : It helps achieve organizational goals such as
profit, market share and sustainability.



5. Integrated activity : marketing integrates production, HR,
research and distribution to deliver value efficiently.
6. Dyanamic and adaptive : marketing changes with trends,
technology, competition, and consumer preference.
7. Social process : marketing impacts society by influencing
consumption patterns, lifestyle and cultural trends.

Q2 What all are the scope and importance of marketing?
Scope of marketing : marketing is not limited to selling. It
includes the entire journey from idea generation to customer
satisfaction.

1. Market research : studying customer behaviour, demand,
competition and market trends
2. Product planning & development : designing products that meet
customer needs / expectations
3. Pricing decision : setting price based on cost, competition
and customer willingness to pay.
4. Customer relationship management : maintaining long term
relationships with customers
5. After sales services : warranty, installation, complaint
handling.


Importance of marketing :

1. Importance to buisness : a) generates revenue & profit
b) helps survive competition
c) builds brand image
d) expands market share
2. Importance to customers : a) provides information
b) offers various choices
c) improves quality through competition
d) ensures better prices
3. Importance to society : a) creates employment
b) promotes innovation
c) enhances standard of living
d) supports economic growth

Q3 Differentiate between needs and wants. Explain how marketing
influences both.
Needs: i) basic human requirements for survival
ii) Essential for life & well-being
iii) limited in number
iv) universal in nature, common to all
v) example : food, water, shelter, clothing

Wants: i) desire for specific products to satisfy needs
ii) not essential for survival
iii) unlimited in number
iv) strongly influenced by marketing
2)



v) example : iphone from Apple, Domino's pizza

-> How marketing influences needs and wants :

1. Marketing does not create basic needs :
-> needs like food, water, shelter, safety already exist
-> marketing only identifies these needs
2. Marketing shapes wants :
-> marketing influences how a need is satisfied
-> it creates preference for a particular product / brand
3. Creates awareness :
-> advertising informs customers about new products
4. Converts wants into demands :
-> When wants are backed by purchasing power, they become
desires / demand
-> eg : discounts / EMI
5. Stimulates new wants :
-> innovation introduces new ways to satisfy needs
-> customers start desiring products they didn't think about
before



Q4 Explain marketing environment
Marketing environment refers to all internal and external
forces that affect a company's marketing decisions and it's
ability to satisfy customers
It includes everything that influces demand, competition, pricing
and business performance

-> features of marketing environment :

1. Dynamic in nature : a) environment constantly changes
b) changes occur due to tech, competition, policies
2. Complex : a) many factors are inter-connected
b) any change in one affects others
3. Uncontrollable : a) most external forces cannot be controlled by the
company
b) businesses can only to environment
4. Relative : impact of environmental factors differs from
company to company
5. Uncertain : future changes are unpredictable
constant supervision is required



Q5 Explain Micro and Macro external environmental factors.
There are mainly two types of marketing environment

1. Internal environment -> refers to all the factors within the
organization that affect its marketing decision
-> generally controllable factors
2. External environment -> consists of forces outside the organization
that influence marketing activities.
These factors are generally uncontrollable.
It is divided into:
a) Micro environment
b) Macro environment

Micro environment : It includes forces that are close to the
company and directly affect it's ability to serve customers.

-> Elements of micro-environment:

1. customers : central focus of marketing
2. competitors : rival firms offering similar products
eg : Apple & Samsung
3. suppliers : provide raw materials & resources
4. publics : media, financial institutions, govt bodies

Macro environment : This involves large societal forces
that affect all businesses indirectly.



Elements of Macro - environments :

1. Political factors : govt. policies, tax rules, trade laws
2. Economic factors : inflation, income levels
3. Social factors : culture, lifestyle, population trends
4. Legal factors : consumer protection laws, labour laws
5. Environment factors : climate change, sustainability regulations
