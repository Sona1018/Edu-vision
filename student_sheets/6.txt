Q1 Define marketing. Explain the nature of marketing
Marketing is the activity, set of institutions and
processes for creating, communicating & delivering
and exchanging offerings that have value for
customers, clients, partners and society at large.

* Nature of Marketing:
1- Customer Oriented
* Marketing begins and ends with the
customer, main aim is to understand
customer needs and satisfy them better
than competitors.



2. Exchange process

* It is based on exchange. Customer gives
money and receives goods or services
of value in return.

3. Value creation

* It focuses on creating superior value
for customers rather than just selling
products.

4. Goal Oriented

* The ultimate goal is profit maximization
through customer satisfaction and long
term relationships.

5. Social & Continuous Process

* Not only about profit but also
social welfare.
* Does not end after selling. It
includes after sales services, feedback
and relationship building.




Q2 What all are scope of marketing & its
importance?

* Scope of Marketing
* It means wide range of activities
covered under marketing :
1. Marketing of goods, physical products like
FMCG, electronics, etc.
2. Marketing of services, banking, services,
insurance, education.
3. Marketing of Ideas, social awareness
campaigns like Swachh Bharat.
4. Marketing of People, celebrities, influencers,
politicians and personal brands.
5. International Marketing, export-import
and global marketing strategies.

* Importance of Marketing
1. Helps to identify and fulfil customer
needs.
2. Increases sales and profits which boosts
demand and revenue
3. Generates employment by giving jobs
in sales, advertising, logistics, etc.
4. Bridges gap between producer and
consumer through distribution channels.
5. Companies improve products based on
market demand which encourages
innovation.
