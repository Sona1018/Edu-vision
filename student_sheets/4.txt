Ques-1  Define Marketing. Explain nature of marketing
along with examples.
Marketing is the process of identifying customer
needs, creating products or services, communicating
their value, and delivering satisfaction to
customers while achieving organizational goals.

Marketing is not only selling or advertising. It
is a complete business philosophy that starts
with customer & ends with customer satisfaction.
Marketing includes:
i) Studying customer needs
ii) Designing suitable products
iii) Pricing properly
iv) Promoting properly
v) Delivering at the right place & time

Marketing = Customer Need → Product Creation → Value → Delivery
Satisfaction
Nature of Marketing -

1. Customer-Oriented: Marketing focuses on fulfilling
customer needs rather than just selling products.
2. Continuous Process: Marketing is ongoing, not a one-
time activity
3. Value Centered: It emphasizes creating value for
customers.




4. Integrated Activity: Includes research, production, pricing
promotion & distribution.
5. Exchange-Based: Involves exchange of goods/services for
money or value.
6. Goal Oriented: Aims at customer satisfaction & organizational
profit.
7. Dynamic: Marketing changes according to trends, technology
and customer behaviour.

Understanding customer Needs:
Understanding customer is the foundation of marketing.
Companies understand customers by using market surveys,
questionnaires, feedback forms, Online reviews, social media
analysis & Focus groups.

Types of customer Needs:

1. Stated Needs - What customer say they want
2. Real Need - What they actually require
3. Latent Needs - Need customers may not express
4. Delight Needs - Extra features that surprise customers

Example - A smartphone user may say They want a good
Camera, but their real need may be clear night photography
& long battery life

Creating Value For Customers:
Value = Benefits - cost
Companies create value by offering quality products, providing

- Fast delivery   - Multiple restaurants   - Discounts

Customer Satisfaction:
Customer satisfaction means how happy a customer is
after using a product or service.
If:
Performance = Expectation → Satisfied
Performance > Expectation → Delighted
Performance < Expectation → Dissatisfied

Why Satisfaction is Important:
• Builds loyalty
• Increases repeat purchase
• Creates positive word of month
• Builds brand image

How companies Identify Problems
Companies must identify problems in customer experience
Common methods-
i) Customer complaints  ii) Review & Ratings
iii) Customer service calls  iv) Surveys

Example-
If many customer complain about late delivery, the
problem is logistics inefficiency.




How companies solve problems
Steps - 1) Identify problem  2) Find cause  3) Develop solution
4) Test solution  5) Implement changes  6) Monitor results.

Example:
Late Delivery → Hire more delivery staff → Improve routing
software → Monitor delivery time.

Example of Marketing in Real Life
Example- Laptop Company

• Company designs low-cost model.
• Price it reasonably
• Promotes through social media
• Sells online & offline
• Provides warranty & service
Result: Students are satisfied → Company earns profit.

Ques-3  Differentiate b/w needs & wants. Explain how
marketing influences both-
Needs-
Needs are the basic necessities of life without
which human survival is not possible. These needs
are common for all human beings and do not
change frequently.

Example of Needs: Food, water, clothing, shelter, Healthcare,
Education.

Wants-
Wants are the desires for specific goods or
services that satisfy needs in a particular way.
Wants are unlimited & influenced by culture,
society, lifestyle and marketing.

Example of Wants: Pizza instead of simple food,
branded clothes instead of normal clothes, iPhone
instead of basic phone.

Basis                Needs                Wants

for survival

Limited/Unlim →    Limited              Unlimited
Influenced by →    Biological Factors   Culture, society, marketing
Example →          Food                 Pizza, Burger
Change with time → Rarely               Frequently

Marketing Environment -
Marketing environment refers to all internal and
external forces, factors & institutions that influences
a company's ability to develop & maintained
successful relationship with customers.
In simpler words, it includes, it includes everything
inside & outside the organization that affects
marketing decisions

'Marketing environment is the sum total of
internal & external factors that influences a
firm's marketing activities & performance.

Characteristics of Marketing Environment-

1. Dynamic - Marketing Environment continuously changes
due to technology, customer tastes, competition
and government policies.
Example
Shift from offline shopping to online shopping.
2. Complex
It consists of many interrelated forces such as social,
economic, political & technological factors.
Example
A change in government tax policy affects product
price & demand.
3. Uncontrollable
Many environmental factors can't be controlled by the
firm.
Example: Inflation or Recession.
