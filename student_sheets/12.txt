Nature of Marketing
-> Nature of marketing explains what marketing is like
, how it works, and its characteristics
Majorly It is customer oriented, value driven, dynamic

1. Customer - Oriented :-
• It focus on need, wants and demand of consumer
• It largely emphasis on how efficiently products are
delivered to customer.
• analyse market, trends, habits and conduct surveys to
understand needs and percerience difference to the
competitors.
Eg:- Amazon focus on fast delivery, easy returns, convenience
2. Continuous Process
Marketing is continuous process and not one time activity
• Begins before production (Research)
• Continues during production and selling (Interaction, Experience)
• Extends even after sales (feedback, services)
• Updation (as market keeps on changing, needs change,
competitors evolve)
Eg:- Mobile brands launch new models every year to stay
relevant and satisfy new needs




3. Integrated activity

Marketing involves coordination of :- (4P's)
product , price , promotion , place
All these activity are connected and support each other
If one fails, effort weakens

Examp :- Zomato
-> Research : studies eating habits
Product : App, filters, subscription
Pricing : discount coupons, free delivery
Promotion : notification , ads , offers
distribution :- delivery partners ,
after - sales :- Ratings , Support .

4. Value Oriented
It aims to create value for customer , not just product
through quality , convenience , Experience
If consumer feels value , sales happen naturally and
business gets edge to competitors
Example Netflix provides personalisation + Entertainment + Convenience
= Value.
5. Exchange - Based .
It means giving something of value and receive something
of value in return where both parties benefits
Consumer -> time / money / attention
Company -> product / quality / Experience / Satisfaction

This mutual transfer is heart of marketing.


Eg :- Burger King => food + quick service + Consistent taste
Consumer => Money + time

Q4 Explain Marketing Environment

Ans -> The marketing enviornment refer to all the internal
and external forces that influence a firm's ability
to develop and maintain successful relationships
with costumers.

Marketing enviorment = All factors outside & inside a
bussiness that affects marketing advices.
These factors are mostly uncontrollable, and markets
must adapt to them rather than control them.

-> Example :-
• A rises in fuel prices :
-> Increases transportation cost
-> Affects product pricing
-> Influences costumer demand.
This changes is part of the marketing enviornment.

-> Characteristics of marketing Enviornment -
(1) Dynamic -
• Enivorment keeps changing continously
• Changes can be slow or sudden.
Example ->
Digital payment increased rapidly after UPI adoption.

(2) Uncertain :
• Difficult to predict future change accurately.
Example ->
COVID 19 drastically changed consumer byying behaviour.



(3) Complex
• Many interrelated factors influence bussiness sim-
ultaneously
Example -
A change in government policy can affect prices,
demands and competition together

(4) Uncontrollable :-
• firms cannot control external forces
• They can only respond and adapt .

types :- individual, business buyers, Govt buyers, Institutional buyers

(2) Suppliers -> Provides raw materials, components or services
Impact => delay -> production issues

4. Technological Environment
