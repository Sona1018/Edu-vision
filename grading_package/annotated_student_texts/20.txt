Ques:1. Define Marketing. Also explain nature of marketing.

(1) Marketing is not just selling a product. it is a
complete cycle of identifying customer needs and wants,
creating product & services to satisfy them , pricing
them suitably , promoting them effectively & delivering
them to customers at the right place & time to
create value & satisfaction.

(2) The main aim of marketing is customer satisfaction
& value creation, & achieving organizational goals.

(3) According to Philip Kotler, marketing is a social &
managerial process by which individuals & groups
obtain what they need & want through creating
& exchanging products & value with others.

(4) American Marketing Association states that marketing
is the activity, set of institutions & processes for
creating, communicating, delivering & exchanging
offerings that have value for customers, clients ,
partners & society at large.

(5) In short, marketing can be summed up as:

Marketing = Understanding Customers

+ Creating Value

+ Delivering satisfaction

(6) It includes activities such as product planning,
pricing , promotion & distribution . These elements are
commonly called the 4 P's of marketing : Product,
Price , Place & Promotion.

(7) Example : Amazon ensures customers save Time , get
variety & trust the platform by following
the marketing process :

├─ Identifies what people want : convenience,
wide choices , low prices , fast delivery.
├─ Developed the product / service : created app &
website offering millions of products enabling
easy returns & customer reviews.
├─ Use competitive pricing method & also provide
with deals, sales & prime benefits.
├─ For promotion , they take assistance of online
advertisements , email offers, festival sales , etc
└─ Ensures availability across cities & timely
home delivery

The nature of marketing explains its basic characteristics,
what it is like & how it functions in real life
business. The prominent characteristics are :

(i) Customer Oriented : • Marketing starts & ends w/ customers.
The main focus is to understand customers' needs &
satisfy them, not just to sell products for profit.
• Example : Amazon focuses on fast delivery , easy
return & customer reviews to improve customer
experience i.e. customer is at center of all activities.

(ii) Continuous Process : • Marketing is not a one-time
activity. It is a continuous & ongoing process
because customer needs, tastes , technology &
competition keep on changing
• Example : Mobile phone brands launch new models
regularly with better cameras , batteries & features
to match changing customer expectations.

(iii) Integrated Activity : All marketing functions like product
price, promotion & place (4 P's) must work together.
In order to be successful , these elements must
be properly coordinated.
• Example : Apple offers high quality products, use premium
pricing, does stylish promotions & sell through
exclusive stores & online channels.

(iv) Value Oriented : • Marketing aims at creating value
for customers , not merely selling products. (Value means
the benefits customers receive compared to cost they
pay , eg: quality , convenience, brand image , etc.
• Example: Netflix provides entertainment , personalised
recommendations , etc which gives high value to customer

(v) Exchange Based : • marketing is based on concept of exchange
where both parties benefit. The customer gives the
money & company gives a product or service.
• Example : when a customer pays a subscription fee to
Netflix & receives access to movies & series , both
sides benefit.

Ques:2. What is the scope of marketing & importance of marketing ?
• The scope of marketing refers to the areas &
activities covered under marketing. It shows how wide
& diverse marketing is today.
• The main scope of marketing includes :

(i) Marketing of Goods : • This includes marketing of
tangible / physical products that can be seen &
touched.
• Companies manufacture goods & market them by
proper branding , packaging , pricing , promotion &
distribution .
• Example : clothes, mobile, TV, cars, food items, etc.

(ii) Marketing of Services : • This includes marketing of
intangible services that cannot be touched but
experienced.
• Service marketing focuses on quality, trust & customer
experience
• Example : banking , education , health care , etc.

(iii) Marketing of Experiences : • Companies market experience
not just products.
• Customers pay for enjoyment, comfort, memorable experience
along w/ basic services.
• Example : Theme parks, luxury hotels , concerts , etc.

(iv) Marketing of Ideas : Marketing is used to spread social
& public ideas for social welfare & awareness.
• Government & NGO's advertise & organise campaigns
to change public behaviour & attitude.
• Example : Swachh Bharat Abhiyaan, Beti Bachao - Beti Padhao,
save water, Road safety awareness , etc.

(v) Digital Marketing : • This refers to marketing through internet
& digital technologies . It helps reach customers quickly
& at low cost through online platforms.
• Example : Email marketing , influencer marketing , etc.

⇒ Importance of Marketing :
marketing is important for businesses, consumers &
society . It connects producers & consumers & helps in
smooth flow of goods & services in the economy.
→ Importance of Marketing to Businesses

(1) Increases Sales & Profits: Marketing creates awareness &
demand for products , which leads to higher sales
& profits. Also support new product launches.
Example : Effective promotions & offers by Amazon
increase customer purchases during sales events.

(2) Builds Brand Image & Goodwill: Marketing helps in
building a strong brand image , trust & reputation
in the market
Example : Apple has created a premium brand image
through consistent marketing & quality.

(3) Helps in Market Expansion: Marketing help business
enter new markets & reach new customer segments
(new countries , cities , age groups , etc)
Example : Reliance Jio expanded rapidly across India
through aggressive marketing strategies.

(4) Provides Competitive Edge: Good marketing helps firm stand
out from competitors through better positioning , branding
& customer experience.

(5) Improves Customer Relationships: Marketing focuses on
long-term relationships , loyalty programs, personalisation
& after sales service not just one-time sales.
This leads to re-purchases & customer retention.

→ Importance of Marketing to Consumers

(1) Satisfaction of needs & wants: Marketing identifies
customer needs & design products to satisfy them effectively

(2) Better Quality & More Choices: Competition among marketers
lead to better quality , more variety , improved features
& customers benefit from wider choices.
(3) Convenience & Time Saving: Marketing ensures products
are easily available at right place & time
(online + offline)

(4) Creates Consumer Awareness : Marketing informs consumers
about product features , prices, uses , new products
thus help make better buying decisions.

(5) Improve Standard of Living: Availability of better
products & services improve comfort, lifestyle & living
standard.

→ Importance of Marketing to Society

(1) Generates Employment : Marketing activities creates jobs
in advertising, sales, digital marketing , etc.

(2) Encourages Innovation & Development : To satisfy
changing customer needs , firms invest in R & D
& introduce improved & innovative products.

(3) Supports Economic Growth : Marketing increases demand ,
production , sales , & consumption , contributing to overall
economic.

(4) Promotes Social welfare & Awareness : Marketing is used
to spread awareness about health , hygiene , environment
& safety

Ques4. Define Marketing Environment.

(1) Marketing environment refers to all the internal &
external factors & forces that influence a firm's
ability to develope & maintain successful relationships
with customers.

(2) Basically marketing envt. refers to all the factors inside
& outside the business that affect marketing decisions
& performance.

(3) Most of these factors are uncontrollable & firms must
adapt to them.

(4) Example: A rise in fuel prices increases transportation
cost & product prices. This change is part of the
marketing environment.

→ Characteristics of Marketing Environment:

(i) Dynamic (Ever Changing) : It keeps on changing
continuously due to changes in technology, customer
preferences, competition & government policies.
Therefore firms must regularly update their
marketing strategies.

Example: Growth of digital payments & online shopping

(ii) Uncertain : Changes in the environment are difficult
to predict accurately. Unexpected events like pandemic,
economic slowdowns or policy changes can affect
businesses suddenly

Example: Covid-19 changed customer behaviour & increased
online shopping , video calls , etc.
(iii) Complex : Marketing environment consist of many
interrelated factors that influence business at the same
time.
A change in one factor can affect prices, demand,
competition & supply together.

(iv) Uncontrollable : External environmental forces are
beyond the control of business firms.
Example : Companies cannot control inflation , laws
social trends or technological changes, they can only
adjust their strategies accordingly.

⇒ Marketing Environment can be classified as:

• Internal : - It consists of factors within the
organisation that influence marketing decisions

- These factors are partially controllable.
- Main components include: Top management,
  organizational structure, company culture, resources
  & internal policies.

• External : - This includes factors outside the
organisation, influencing the decisions.

- These include technological advancements, customer,
  regulatory changes, etc.
- These are uncontrollable so firms must adapt.

Ques5. Explain the Micro & Macro external environmental
factors.

The External Marketing Environment can be further
classified into Micro & Macro Environment.

⇒ MICRO ENVIRONMENT

Micro external factors are those factors close to the
company that directly affects its ability to serve
customers & create value. These include:

(1) Customers : • They are the center of all marketing
activities. They include individual customers,
business buyers, government buyers, etc.

• Changes in customer's need, preferences, income, lifestyle
directly affects product design, pricing & promotion.

• Example : Byju's design courses based on student needs.

(2) Suppliers : • They provide raw material, components,
services required for production.

• Delay, price increase or poor quality by suppliers
lead to production delay, higher product prices,
poor quality products respectively.

• Example : Global chip shortages affected automobile
& electronics industry.

(3) Competitors : • They offer similar products & services &
fight for same customers.

• Competition influences pricing strategies, product features,
innovation, etc

• Example : competition from Jio forced others to reduce
data prices & introduce better plans.

(4) Market Intermediaries : • They help in promoting, selling
& distributing products. It includes wholesalers, retailers,
transport & advertising agencies, etc.

• Efficient intermediaries improve availability & reach.

• Example : Amazon acts as an intermediary b/w buyers
& sellers.

(5) Publics : • groups that have an interest in or impact
on company's ability to achieve its objective.

• Example : Positive media builds brand image while
negative publicity damages reputation.
→ MACRO ENVIRONMENT

macro external factors are large , broad, uncontrollable
forces that affect not just one company but the
entire industry & market. These include:

(1) Demographic Factors : • Population characteristics like
age , gender, income , education , family size affect
product demand & market size.

• Example : Young population ↑ demand for smartphones
& online education.

(2) Economic Factors : • Economic conditions like income level,
inflation , interest rates , economic growth affect the
purchasing power.

• Example : High inflation reduces spending on luxury items.

(3) Political & Legal Factors : • Government policies , tax laws,
labor laws , trade policies & regulations affect
businesses.

• Companies must follow legal rules in pricing ,
advertising & product safety.

(4) Technological Factors : • changes in technology trends
affects products, production & promotion.

• They lead to new products & services, online marketing,
automation & digital payments.

• Example : Instagram changed how companies promote
products & interact w/ customers.

(5) Environmental Factors : •They include natural
resources & ecological factors like climate change,
pollution , envt. laws resource scarcity , etc

• companies must adopt eco-friendly packaging &
sustainable practices.
(6) Social & Cultural Factors : • Includes social values ,
lifestyle , beliefs , customs & attitudes of people.

• Changes in lifestyle influence product demand.

• Example : Health awareness ↑ demand for organic
food & fitness products.
