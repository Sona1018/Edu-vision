Q1) Define marketing. Explain the nature of marketing.
Marketing is the process of identifying, anticipating,
and satisfying customer needs and wants through the
creation, promotion, distribution and exchange of goods
and services.

NATURE OF MARKETING

The nature of marketing can be explained through
the following characteristics:-

1. Customer Oriented: Marketing focuses on understanding
customer needs and delivering satisfaction
better than competitors.

2. Goal Oriented: The primary goal is to achieve organisation
objectives such as profit, growth, or market
share.

3. Exchange Process: Marketing is based on the concept of
exchange - goods and services are exchanged
for money or other value

4. Continuous Process: It is not a one time activity, it involves
continuous research, planning, implementa-

5. Integrated Purpose: It integrates various functions like
advertising, sales, distribution etc.

6. Dynamic in Nature: Marketing practices change according to
market trends, tech, competition, etc.

7. Value Creation: It creates value by offering the right product
at the right price, place & promotion.

marketing influences both.

| Basis       | Needs                   | Wants                               |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Meaning     | Basic human requirement | Desires for specific products       |

Marketing does not create needs, but its solves and
satisfies them effectively.

Marketing Influence on wants

Marketing strongly influences wants by:
• Advertising
• Branding
• Celebrity endorsements
• Social media promotion

Q4 Explain marketing environment
Marketing environment refers to all internal & external
forces that affect the marketing activities of a company

It consist of factors that influence the company's ability
to build and maintain successful relationship with
customers.

Features
1. Complex - Includes multiple forces
2. Dynamic - Continuously changing
3. Uncontrollable - many factors cannot be controlled by the firm
4. Interrelated - factors are connected and affect each other

Components
1. Internal Environment
2. External Environment

[Q_START: Q5 | Type: Analytical_Explanation | Weight: 20.0%]
Q5 Explain micro & macro environmental factors.
Micro Environment - consists of factors that are close to the
company and directly affect its ability to
serve customers.

Elements of Micro Environment-
1. Customers- The target market of the company
2. Competitors - Other businesses offering similar products.
3. Suppliers - Provide raw materials & resources
4. Intermediaries- Distributors, wholesalers & retailers
5. Public - Media, govt bodies & local communities

Macro Environment - includes larger societal forces that
indirectly affect business operations.

Elements of Macro Environment-
1. Economic Factors - Inflation, income levels, unemployment rate
2. Political & Legal factors- Govt. laws, policies, taxation.
3. Social & Cultural Factors- Customs, traditions, values, etc.
4. Technological Factors - Innovation, automation, digital
   transformation.
5. Demographic Factors- Population size, age structure, education
6. Natural Factors- Climate change, environmental regulations
