Q1. Define marketing and nature of marketing

Ans. Marketing is the process of identifying customers
needs and wants , creating value , and delivering
satisfaction profitably.

 According to American Marketing Association , 
 ""Marketing is the activity , set of institutions , 
 and processes for creating , communicating , 
 delivering , and exchanging offerings that have 
 values for customers , clients , partners , & 
 society at large "".

 Marketing = Understanding customers + Creating value + 
             delivering satisfaction .

 Understanding customer : It involves studying 
                          customers needs , preferences 
 income level , behaviour , and expectations through 
 market research and feedback.

 Creating value : buisnesses design products or service 
                  that solve customer problems 
 better than competitors. Value can be in the 
 form of quality , price , convience or innovation.

Q2. What all are scope of marketing and importance
of marketing.

Ans. Scope of marketing acts as an umbrella to
marketing that covers all basic concepts. It
includes marketing of both tangible and
intangible offerings and has expanded further
with the growth of technology.

1. Marketing of Goods; This includes marketing of
physical products such as clothes
mobiles , cars , food items and household goods.
Activities like product design , packaging , branding
pricing and distribution are involved.
2. Marketing of services ; Services are intangible in
nature and include banking
insurance , education , healthcare , tourism , transport
and hospitality services. Marketing focuses on
quality , trust , customer experience and
satisfaction.

3. Marketing of experience ; Many organisations market
experience rather than just
products or services. For example , theme parks ,
movies , concerts , luxury hotels and travel
destinations provide memorable customer
experience.
4. Marketing of ideas ; Marketing is also used to promote
social political and public welfare ideas , such as
awareness campaigns for health.



Real company example ; Apple Inc.

Step 1; Understanding customer : Apple studies customer
preferences for stylish design, high performance
privacy, and smooth user experience.

Step 2; Creating value; Apple creates innovative products
like iphones and Macbooks with premium design
strong security and easy-to-use software.

Step 3; Delivery value; Products are delivered through
Apple stores, online platforms and authorised
retailers with proper customer service and
warranty.

Nature of marketing;

1. Customer-centric; Marketing focuses on customer
needs and satisfaction. All
business activities revolve around the customer.
2. Continuous Process; Marketing is not a one-time
activity. It continues even
after the sale through feedback, service, &
relationship building.
3. Integrated Activity; Marketing involves coordination
of various activities like product development,
pricing, promotion & distribution. All departments
work together

Q4. Explain marketing environment.

Ans. The marketing environment consists of all internal
and external forces that affects an company's
ability to build and maintain successfull
relationships with customers.

```
 It Includes factors that influence ;

 1. Product decisions
 2. Pricing strategies
 3. Promotion activities
 4. Distribution channels
 5. Customer satisfaction

 In marketing managements , no organisation
 operates in isolation. Every firm is surrounded
 by dynamic forces that continuously affects its
 performance.

 Characteristics of marketing environment.

 1. Dynamic nature ; It keeps changing continuously

 2. Complex ; Many factors operate simultaneously

 3. Uncontrollable ; External forces cannot be fully
                     controlled
 4. Inter-related ; One factor affects another.

 5. Uncertain ; Future changes can not be predicted
                accurately.

```



Components of marketing environment;

The marketing environment is divided into;

1. Internal environment;
Factors within the organisation such as;

a) Organisational structure

b) Company culture

c) Finance

d) Production capacity

e) Research and development

f) Human resource

The above factors are controllable and directly
influence marketing strategy.

External environment;

These are the forces that influence marketing
decisions.

It is divided into ; i. Macro environment
ii. Micro environment
