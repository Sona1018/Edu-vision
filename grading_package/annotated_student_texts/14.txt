Q1. Define Marketing and Nature of marketing .
Differentiate between needs and wants . Explain how
Marketing influence the both.

✪ Marketing = understanding + creating + Delivering
customers value Satisfaction

Acc. to Philip Kotler -
Marketing is a social and managerial process by which
individuals & groups obtain what they need and want
through creating & exchanging products and value
with others.

Acc. to American Marketing Association(AMA):-
Marketing is the activity, set of institutions & processes
for creating, communicating, delivering and exchanging
offerings that have value for customers, clients,
partners and society at large.

✪ Understanding customer Needs
ex-> Zomato studied that urban customers wants
convenience & fast food delivery. It identified the
need for quick service and easy ordering.

✪ Delivering customer Value
ex-> Netflix delivers value through : Personalised
recommendations
-> ad free entertainment
-> Content anytime, anywhere

✪ Costomer satisfaction
ex-> if Zomato delivers food before promised time ->
mer feels delighted.

Nature of Marketing
1 Customer Generic -> focus is on customer satisfaction
rather than just profit.
2 Continous process -> Marketing is on going because
customer preferences and technology keep changing.
3 Integrated Activity -> Involves coordination of product
Price, place and promotion (4P's)
4 Value Oriented -> Marketing aims to create
superior value for customers.
5 Exchange Based -> Marketing is based on exchange
Money for goods/services.

Explain Marketing environment give its definition
and characteristics with examples.

-> Marketing environment = All forces around a business
that affect it's marketing decisions.

-> Marketing environment consists of the actors and
forces outside and inside the organisation that affect
managements ability to built and maintain successful
relationships with customers.

✪ Characteristics of Marketing Environment

1 Dynamic in Nature -
-> The Marketing environment is constantly changing
✪ Technology changes, customer preferences change,
Market trends change.
ex -> Growth of digital payments and online shopping
changed traditional retail buisnesses.

2 Uncertain -
-> future changes in the environment are difficult
to predict.
ex -> During Covid-19 consumer buying behaviour suddenly
shifted towards online platforms & essential goods

3 Complex -
-> many factors affect buisness simultaneously and they
are interconnected.
ex -> A change in government tax policy may increase
product price, reduce demand and affect profits at
the same time.

4 Uncontrollable
-> External factors cannot be controlled by companies
Rises in fuel prices increases transportation
costs which affects product pricing.
4

Types of Marketing Environment.

✪ Internal Environment -
-> factors within the organization such as
top management, company culture, financial
resources.
ex -> if management focus on innovation, the
company will invest more in research and
development.

✪ External Environment
1 Micro Environment
factors close to the company:
• Customers
• Suppliers
• Competitors
• Marketing intermediaries.
ex -> if suppliers increase raw material prices, the
company's production cost increases.

2 Macro Environment
Large external forces affecting the entire industry:
• Demographic factors (age, income, population)
• Economic factors (e.g. Inflation, income levels)
• Political & legal factors (laws)
• Technological factors (new invention)
