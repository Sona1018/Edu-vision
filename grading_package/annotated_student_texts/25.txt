Q1. Define marketing. Explain nature of marketing.
Ans. Marketing is the process of identifying, anticipating, 
and satisfying customer needs and wants
 profitably. According to AMA, Marketing is the
 activity, set of institutions, and processes for 
creating, communicating, delivering, and exchanging offers 
that have value for customers, clients, partners
 and society at large. Marketing is not just selling
 it includes understanding customer needs, designing 
products, pricing them, promoting them, and 
distributing them effectively.

Nature of Marketing-

1 Customer-Oriented: It focuses on understanding custome
 needs and satisfying them better than competitors.
 Customer satisfaction is the main goal.

2 Goal-Oriented: It aims to achieve organizational goals
 such as profit, growth, and market share.

3. Exchange Process: It involves exchange of goods/services
 for money or value. Both buyer & seller should
 benefit from the transaction.

4 Integrated Process: It integrates various activities like 


product design, pricing, promotion, and distribution (4Ps) 
All departments work together to satisfy customers.

5 Continuous Process: Marketing is ongoing. Companies
 continuously research, improve, and adapt to 
market changes.

6 Creates Utility: It creates different utility-
• Form Utility- Changing raw materials into finished goods.
• Place Utility- Making goods available at the right place.
• Time Utility- Making goods available at the right time.
• Possession Utility- Transferring ownership to buyer.

Q4. Explain marketing environment.
Ans Marketing environment refers to all internal & external 
factors that affect a company's ability to serve its c
ustomers successfully. These factors influence 
marketing decisions, strategies & performance. The 
marketing environment is divided into-
1. Internal         2. External


1. Internal Environment- it includes factors within the
 company that affect marketing activities. These factors
 are generally controllable.

1 Company Objectives- Marketing strategies depend on 
company goals. Ex-Tesla focuses on sustainability, 
so its marketing highlights clean energy.

2 Management & Employees- Skilled employees & effective 
management improve marketing success.

3 Financial Resources- Budget determines advertising, promotion,
 expansion capacity.

4 Company Cultur- Innovative culture leads to creative 
marketing strategies. Ex- Apple promotes innovation and
 premium branding.

2. External Environment- It includes factors outside the 
company that influence marketing. These are generally
uncontrollable. It is divided into- Macro & Micro

Q5. Explain Micro and Macro Environment factors.
Ans 1. Micro Environment - Includes factors that are close to
 the company & directly affect its day-to-day
 marketing activities.

1 Customers-> Most important factor. Businesses exist to 
satisfy customers. Ex- Zara frequently updates collections based 
on customer preferences.

2 Competitors-> Companies must analyze competitor pricing
promotion & strategies. Ex- Coca-Cola competes closely with Pepsi.

3 Suppliers-> Suppliers provide raw materials. Delay or
 price increase affects production.

4 Intermediaries-> Wholesalers, retailers, and distributors help
 in delivering products to customers. Ex- Amazon acts 
 as an intermediary between sellers and buyers.

5 Public-> Media, government agencies,& local communities 
influence company image.

2. Macro Environment- Includes broad external factors that affect
 business & markets.

1 Demographic Environment:-> Population size, age, gender, 
education, income.

2. Economic Environment-> Inflation, recession, interest 
rate, income level. Ex- During economic 
slowdown, luxury brands like Rolex may 
face reduced demand.

3. Political & Legal Environment-> government policies, laws, 
labour laws, regulations.

4. Technological Environment-> Innovation, automation, digital 
transformation. Ex- Smartphone growth helped companies 
like Samsung expand globally.

5. Social & Cultural Environment:-> Beliefs, values, traditions, 
lifestyle, changes.
