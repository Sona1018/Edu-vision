Ques2. Explain Marketing Environment. Give its definition
and characteristics with example.

Marketing environment refers to all internal and external
factors that affect marketing decisions. It includes
forces that influence a company's ability to serve
customers and maintain successful relationships with
customers.
It includes factors that influence marketing decisions,
customer demand, pricing , promotion and distribution

Characteristics of marketing environment

① Dynamic (ever changing)
The marketing environment keeps changing continuously
eg- Technology changed from keypad phones to
smartphones. Companies that adapted survived

② Complex
It consist of many interrelated factors
eg- Inflation affects purchasing power, which affects
demand which affects production and
pricing

③ Uncontrollable
External environmental factors cannot be controlled
by companies
eg- Government increases tax on tobacco products
companies must adjust pricing.

④ Relative
The environment differs from business to business
Eg- Rainfall is important for agriculture companies
but not much for software companies

⑤ Opportunity and Threat Based
It can create both opportunities and risks
eg- Growth of online shopping created opportunity
for e-commerce companies but threat for
small retail shops.

⑥ Customer Oriented
Marketing environment mainly revolves around
customers needs and behaviour
eg- Increased health awareness led to higher demand
for organic food.

Ques3- Explain micro and macro external environmental
factors.

Marketing environment is divided into two categories

① Micro Environment
② Macro Environment

① Micro Environment
It consist of factors that are close to the company and
directly affect its ability to serve customers.

These factors have direct and immediate impact
on business operations.

1. Company - Includes internal department like
   production , finance , HR and marketing.
   eg- If production department delays manufactur
   ing, Marketing cannot supply product on time

2. Marketing Intermediaries - Distributors, wholesalers,
   retailers who help sell products.
   eg- Amazon acts as intermediary between sellers
   and customers.

3. Suppliers - Provide raw materials as input.
   eg- If chip suppliers delay supply mobile
   companies cannot produce smartphone.

4. Competitors - Other companies offering similar
   products.
   eg- Pepsi and Coca-Cola compete in
   the beverage market.

5. Customer - The target market who buys the
   product. eg- If customers prefer eco-friendly
   products, company must adapt.

6. Publics - Media, Finance institute, Government,
   local communities.
eg- Negative media publicity can reduce brand reputation

② Macro Environment

Macro environment consists of large societal forces
that affects the entire industry and economy
These factors are indirect and uncontrollable.

elements in macro environment:

1. Demographic Environment:
   Population size, age, gender, education.
   eg- Growing youth population increases demand
   for fashion and gadgets.

2. Economic Environment
   Income level, inflation, recession, unemployment.
   eg- During recession, people reduce luxury
   purchases.

3. Social and Cultural Environment
   Beliefs, values, customs, traditions
   Eg- Growing health awareness increases demand
   for organic food.

4. Technological Environment
   Innovation and technology development
   eg- Online banking apps replace traditional banking methods.

5. Political and Legal Environment
   Government policies, Tax, laws, regulations.
   eg- Increase in GST affects production price.

demand including:

- Packaging
- Branding

- cost of production
- demand and supply
- competition
- customer purchasing power

④ Promotion Activities

- Advertising
- Sales promotion
- Personal selling
- Public relations
  Promotion helps create awareness and demand

competition

Ques Difference between need and want. How marketing
influence them Both?
- Needs are the basic necessities required for human
  survival and well being. They are essential and
  limited in nature
- eg- Food, water, shelter, clothing and safety

* Wants are the desires for specific products or
  services that satisfies needs in a particular
  way
* They are influenced by culture, society and personal
  preferences.
* eg- Pizza instead of simple food, branded cloths

| Basis         | Needs                           | Wants                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Meaning       | Basic requirements for survival | Desires for specific satisfaction |
| Nature        | essential                       | non-essential                     |
| Origin        | Biological and psycholo- gical  | Social and cultural influence     |
| Number        | Limited                         | Unlimited                         |
| Changeability | Do not change early             | change frequently                 |
| eg            | Hunger (need)                   | Burger (want)                     |

shape how needs are satisfied

① Identifying needs

② Transforming needs into wants

③ Creating awareness

④ Influencing consumer preference

⑤ Changing lifestyle and trends

⑥ Adding value perception

⑦ Encouraging demands

specific desires shaped by culture and environment.

influences wants by informing, persuading and

how needs are satisfied.
