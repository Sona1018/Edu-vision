Q1 Define Marketing and write a brief note on it, Stating  Examples.

• Marketing is the process of identifying customers needs, developing products / services to satisfy those needs, pricing them appropriately, promoting them effectively, and making them available.

• Marketing = Understanding + Creating + Delivering
　　　　　　　　　customer     value      satisfaction
 
• Example : Zomato → not only sell food delivery
　　　　　　　　 → Studies eating habit
　　　　　　　　 → identify convenience as a need
　　　　　　　　 → offer fast delivery, discounts,
　　　　　　　　　 ratings and personalization.

• NATURE OF MARKETING

① Customer Centric
→ focus on customer satisfaction, not just
　profit
Ex: Amazon → fast delivery, easy returns,
　　　customer reviews.

② Continuous Process
→ customer preference changes
→ competition changes
→ Technology evolves
Ex: Mobile → new launch each year



③ Integrated Activity
→ involves coordination of
　• Product • Price • Promotion • Place
Ex: Apple → premium pricing, match its
　product quality & brand image

④ Value oriented

- value for customer, not just product
Ex: Netflix → entertainment + convenience +
personalization = value

⑤ Exchange - Based:
→ involves exchange
　• customer gives money
　• company gives product/service

Both parties are at benefit.

Q2- what all are scope of marketing and importance of marketing?

Ans Scope of Marketing

→ It refers to all activities involved in moving goods and services from producers to consumers and satisfying customer needs.

① Product- Planning and Development

• marketing starts before product is made. It includes
　↳ what- product to make
　↳ features, design, packaging, branding

Ex: Apple marketing its newly launched phone
　　even before launching

② Buying and Assembling

• marketers help in procuring raw materials or
goods needed for selling.

Ex: Supermarket- buys veggies from farmers
　　to sell to customers.



③ Selling

• convince customers to buy the product
　through sales technique

Ex: Car showroom gives test drive to help
　　customer decide.

④ Pricing

• determine value of product in monetary terms.
　keeping in mind competition, customer
　demand

Ex: Premium brands charge high price to
　　reflect superior quality and status.

⑤ Promotion

• inform and persuading customers through
　advertising, sales promotion, personal
　selling and public relations.

Ex: T.V ad. promoting new detergent



Importance of Marketing.

① Satisfaction of customers needs.

→ helps identify and fulfil needs and wants of
　customers

Eg: low sugar products for health-conscious

② Increase in Sales and Profit

→ effective marketing attracts more customers
　leading to higher sales and profitability
Eg: promotional discounts during festive seasons
　boost sales.

③ Create Awareness.

→ inform about new products and services available
　in the market
Eg: Advertisements introducing new mobile app.

④ Better Living Standard

→ provides a variety of quality goods and
　services improving lifestyle and convenience
Eg: home delivery services saves time and
　effort.



⑤ Support Economic Development

→ marketing increases demand leading to higher
　production, employment and industrial income
Ex: MNC expanding and creating jobs

⑥ Building Brand Image

→ helps create a strong positive image of a
　brand in minds of consumers. A well -
  recognised brand gains trust, credibility,
  and loyalty, making it easier to
　retain customers.

⑦ Encouraging Innovation

→ marketing research often identifies gaps in
　the market or changing customer
　preferences. This motivates business to innovate
　improve product, or introduce new solutions.

⑧ Strengthening Customer Relationship

→ Modern marketing focuses on engaging
　with customers through personalized
　communication, social media ,and 
  loyalty program.

Marketing Influences Needs and Wants

① Marketing

→ marketing can't create needs, because needs are inherent to human beings. It just help to identify, understand and satisfy them effectively Eg: milk, eggs, bread.

→ Marketing influence wants by presenting different ways to satisfy needs.

Eg: A person needs food but advertisement influence them to prefer McDonald's or Domino's

② Branding and Promotion

→ Through advertising and branding, market builds preferences for a product

Eg: Prefering Nike for shoes

③ Product Differentiation

→ creates variety and alternatives turning simple need into specific wants

Eg: need to transportation to luxury car due to promotion

④ Lifestyle Influence

→ link product with status, fashion, and lifestyle shaping consumer's wants. Eg: Smartwatch influencing consumer beyond basic needs.



⑤ Consumer Education

→ Marketing educates customers about the benefits, uses and values of a product
　helping them recognize needs they
　may not have been aware of.
Ex: Ads

⑥ Trend Setting

→ Marketing Introduces new trends and innovations
　encouraging consumers to desire products
　that align with modern lifestyle
Ex: fashion brands.

⑦ Emotional Appeal

Marketing appeals to emotions - happiness,
pride, security which can shape both
needs and wants by linking products
to feelings.
Ex: Car showing safety

⑧ Convenience and Accessibility

Marketing highlights how products save
time, effort or improve convenience
turning practical needs into preferred
choices. Ex: Online grocery delivery service.

Q4. Explain marketing environment

→Marketing Environment refers to all the internal and external forces that influence a firm's ability to develop and maintain successful relationship with customers.

These factors are mostly uncontrollable and marketers must adapt to them rather than control them.

Example:
- rise in fuel price
- increase in transportation cost
- product pricing
- influences consumer demand

→ Characteristics of Marketing Environment

① Dynamic → keeps changing

Ex: digital payments increased after UPI

② Uncertain → difficult to predict future changes accurately

Ex: COVID-19 changed consumer buying behaviour

③ Complex → many interrelated factors influence business simultaneously

Ex: change in govt. policy affect price,
　　demand & consumption together.



(4) Uncontrollable → firms can't control external f
                                              → only respond and adapt
