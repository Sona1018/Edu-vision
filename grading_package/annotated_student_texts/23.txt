Q1. Explain marketing and define nature of 
marketing.

Marketing is the process of identifying, anticipating 
and satisfying customer needs and wants 
through the creation, promotion, distribution 
and exchange of goods and services at a profit.

According to philip kotler, "marketing is a 
social and managerial process by which 
individuals and groups obtain what they need
 and want through creating and exchanging 
product and value to them in a way that 
it benefits both customers and organization".

- Nature of Marketing -

• Marketing is customer-oriented
The main focus is to identify customer needs, 
preferences, and expectations and then design
products accordingly.
Here, companies conduct market research, 
long-term relationships are emphasized.

• Marketing is a continuous process
It is not a one time activity or limited to selling.
It includes : product development, pricing, 
promotion, distribution, customer feedback.

• Marketing is a social process
It operates within society and affects social 
behaviour, culture, and lifestyle.
It influences consumption patterns, reflects 
social values and follows ethical standards.

• Marketing is both Art & Science
Science : uses data analysis, market research, 
consumer behaviour studies.
Art : creativity in advertising, branding 
strategies, emotional appeal.

Q2. What are scope of marketing and 
importance of marketing.

Marketing is a broad concept that goes beyond 
selling products.

- Scope of Marketing

• Goods Marketing
It includes marketing of tangible products like 
- consumer goods (clothes, product), industrial
 goods (machinery, raw materials)
Ex: Apple Inc. markets phone, laptop and others.

• Services Marketing
It includes marketing of services like banking, 
insurance, education, tourism etc.
Ex: Airlines, banks, hospitals, safety, customer experience.

• Event Marketing
It includes marketing of special events like 
sports event, cultural program, trade fair etc.
Ex: IPL is highly marketed to attract viewers and 
sponsors.

• Person Marketing
It includes individuals like- film stars, politician
influencers and sports personality.
Ex: Political campaign promotes candidates (election)

- Importance of Marketing

• To business organizations
It increases sales and revenue, builds brand
 image, helps in facing competition, guides
 production.

• To customers
It satisfies needs and wants ; provides info 
about price, features, uses ; improves standard
 of living.

• To modern economy
It facilitates exchange, supports global trade, 
create utility like form, place, time, etc.

• To society
It generates employment, encourages innovation
and promotes economic development.

Q3. Differentiate between needs and wants.
Explain how marketing influences both.

Needs are basic human requirement necessary
for survival and well-being. They are universal
and limited in number.
Eg: Food, Water, Shelter, Clothing, Safety.
According to Maslow, human needs can be
arranged in hierarchy such as physiological
needs, safety needs, social need, esteem needs.

Whereas wants are desires for specific
products or services that satisfy needs. They are
shaped by culture, lifestyle, personality etc.
Eg: needs- food but wants- pizza, burger, etc.

- Marketing influence

• Marketing does not create basic needs.
Eg: Human naturally need food, marketing cannot
create hunger.

• Marketing expands awareness of needs
Eg: health campaign make people aware of fitness,
insurance adv. highlights need of financial
security.

• Marketing shapes consumer preferences
Eg: need-communication but want - iphone instead
of android phone or other.
Done by companies like Apple & Samsung.

• Marketing creates emotional and psychological
appeal
It appeals to status, prestige, lifestyle & acceptance.
Ex: luxury brands create want based on status
rather than basic need or necessity.

Q4. Explain Marketing Environment.

The marketing environment refers to all internal
and external factors that affect an organization
ability to build and maintain successful
relationship with customers.

It includes that influence marketing decision,
customer demand, business strategies, and
overall performance.
In simple words, marketing environment 
all forces surrounding a business that impact
its marketing activities.

- Internal Environment (Controllable)
Company Culture, Structure, Resources,
Operational Capabilities.

- Micro Environment (External but close)
Close to company that directly affect ability to
serve customers.

- Macro Environment (External, Uncontrollable)
Broader societal forces that affects entire micro-
environment.

• Micro Environment- Consist of factors that are
close to the company and directly affects its
operations.

- Suppliers- provides raw materials and resources.
Reliable supplier ensures smooth
operations, inc
in supplier affects pricing strategy.

- Marketing Intermediaries- It includes wholesalers,
retailers, transport company, advertising agency.

- Consumers- It influences markets like
Business, government and international.

- Competitors- Influences price, quality & strategy
company must monitor competitor activities,
develop an advantage. Eg: Pepsi & CocaCola
constantly compete through advertising & branding.

- Demographic- Refers to population such as age,
gender, income, education, occupation, etc.
Eg: Young people increase demand of gadgets.

interest rates, unemployment.
Eg: During recession, customer gives up on luxury items.

pollution, resource availability, regulations.

E-commerce, Digital Payment, Social Media, AI.
Eg: Amazon has transformed retail marketing.
