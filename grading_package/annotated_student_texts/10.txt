Q- Define marketing and write about nature of it.
o Marketing is the process of identifying
customer needs, developing products/
services to satisfy those needs, pricing
them appropriately, promoting them
effectively, and making them available
at the right place and time

o Marketing = Understanding customers + creating
                value + Delivering satisfaction

o Example -
Zomato doesn't just sell food delivery
It studies eating habits, identifies
convenience as need and offers fast
delivery, discounts, ratings and
personalization.
That is marketing and not just selling

o The nature of marketing explains its
basic characteristics

o Key Characteristics -
1 Customer - centric
Marketing starts and ends with the
customer. Focus is on customer satisfaction


not just profit
Example- Amazon focuses on fast delivery,
easy returns and customer reviews.

2 continuous Process
marketing is an ongoing activity
not a one time effort. customer
prefences changes, competition changes
and technology evolves.
Example- mobile phone brands launch
new models every year based on changing
needs.

3. Integrated Activity
Marketing involves coordination of
product, price, promotion and place.
All activities must work together.
Example- Apple's premium pricing matches
its product quality and brand image.

Value - Oriented
Marketing aims to create value for
customers, not just products.
Example- Netflix provides entertainment,
convenience and personalization

Exchanged Based
marketing involves exchange. customer


gives money, company gives product/service.
Both parties benefit.

Q Differentiate between needs and wants.
Explain how marketing influences both
of them.
o Needs
Needs are the basic human requirements
that are essential for survival, growth, and
well being. They arise naturally and are
not created by marketers Every human
being has certain fundamental needs such
as food, shelter, clothing safety and belongingness

o Characteristics
-> Basic and fundamental - needs are essential
for survival
-> Exist in every human being - All individuals
have similar basic needs
-> Remain constant over time - Human needs
do not change frequently. For example,
people always need food and safety



o Wants
Wants are the desires for specific
products or services. Unlike needs,
wants are influenced by external factors
such as culture, income, lifestyle,
education and personal preferences.
marketing plays a role in shaping and
influencing wants.

o Characteristics of wants
-> Unlimited - Human wants are never ending
and keep increasing
-> Vary from person to person - Different
individuals have different preferences
-> Influenced by society and culture - Social
environment and trends affect wants.
-> Changed by marketing efforts - Advertising
and branding can create new desires

o Basis      Needs             Wants
Nature    Basic & essential  Desire based
Creation  Natural            Influenced by factors
Example   Food               Burger, pizza
Stability Stable             Changeable

o Example-
Need - Transportation, want - Royal Enfield bike
Need - communication, want - iphone 15


Influence of marketing on needs
Needs are basic human requirements.
These exist naturally in every human
being. Although marketing cannot create
needs, it identifies existing customer needs,
designs products to satisfy those needs,
improves convenience and accessibility.

o Influence of marketing on wants
Marketing shapes wants by -
-> Advertising - Advertisements create desire by
showing how a product improves life.
-> Branding - Strong brand create emotional
attachment
-> Packaging - Attractive packaging influences
buying decisions
-> Lifestyle Positioning - products are associated
with a particular lifestyle
-> Social media & influencing - Influencers
create trends and affect purchasing preferences

[Q_START: Q4 | Type: Analytical_Explanation | Weight: 20.0%]
Q- Explain marketing environment
o The marketing environment refers to all
the internal and external forces that
influence an organization ability to
develop and maintain successful
relationship with customers.


In simple words, all internal and external
factors that affect marketing decisions
The marketing environment determines
pricing decisions, product development,
promotion strategies and distribution methods

o Example
If there is a rise in fuel prices
-> Transportation cost increases
-> Production cost may rise
-> Product prices may increase
-> Customer demand may reduce
This change directly affects business operations
and marketing decisions. Therefore, it is
part of the marketing decisions

o Characteristics of marketing environment
-> Dynamic
The marketing environment is constantly
changing. Changes may be gradual or
sudden
Ex- Digital payments increased rapidly after
UPI adoption, changing how businesses
operate and receive payments

-> Uncertain
Future changes in the environment are
difficult to predict accurately.


Ex- COVID 19 drastically changed consumer
buying behaviour, increasing online
shopping and reducing physical store
visits

-> Complex
The marketing environment consists of
many interrelated factors that affect
business simultaneously A single change
can impact multiple areas.
Ex- A change in government policy
can affect production cost, pricing etc.

-> Uncontrollable
Organisations cannot control external
forces such as economic conditions, political
decisions, technological changes. They can
only monitor these changes and adjust
their strategies accordingly.

o Types of marketing environment
-> Internal marketing environment
-> External marketing environment

Internal Marketing Environment
The internal environment consists of factors
within the organisation that influence



marketing decisions. These factors are
partially controllable because they exist
within the organization.
Example- company policies, production capacity

o External marketing environment
The external environment includes factors
outside the organization that influence
marketing activities. These factors are
uncontrollable and organization must
adapt to them.
It is divided into
-> Micro environment
-> Macro environment

Q- Explain micro and macro external
environment.
o External marketing environment is
divided into-
Micro environment, macro environment.

o Micro environment
The micro environment includes factors
that are close to the company and
directly affect its ability to serve customers
These factors have a direct and
immediate impact on business operations


Elements of micro environment
Company
Different departments like finance,
production, R&D and marketing must
coordinately effectively for successful marketing

Suppliers
Suppliers provide raw materials and
components. Any delay, shortage or
price increase effects production and
pricing

-> Customers
Customers are the most important part of
the micro environment. Their preferences,
buying power, and behaviour directly
influence sales.

-> Competitors
Competitors influence pricing, product quality
and promotional strategies.

-> Marketing Intermediaries
These include wholesalers, retailers, distributors
and agents who help in delivering products
to customers.



-> Public
Any group that has an interest in the
company such as media, financial
institutions and local communities

o Macro environment
The macro environment consists of
broader forces that affect not only one
company but the entire industry and
economy These forces are indirect but
powerful. They shape long-term opportunities
and threats for businesses

o Elements of macro environment.
-> Demographic Environment
Relates to population size, age, gender, income el

-> Economic Environment
Includes inflation, income levels, interest rates etc

-> Political and legal Environment
Government policies, laws, regulation
and political stability affect business
operations

-> Technological Environment
Technological advancements create new



opportunities and challenges.

-> Social and cultural Environment
Values, beliefs, customs and lifestyle trends
influence customer behaviour

-> Natural Environment
Includes climate change, natural resource,
environmental regulations
