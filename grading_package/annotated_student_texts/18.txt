Ques1:- Define marketing and nature of marketing. Differentiate between
 needs and wants. Explain how marketing influences the both

Ans Marketing refers to the process of understanding
customers, creating value and delivering satisfaction.

According to Philip Kotler:-
Marketing is a social and managerial process by which
individuals and groups obtain what they need and want
through creating and exchanging products and value with
others.

According to American Marketing Association (AMA):-
Marketing is the activity, set of institutions and process
for creating, communicating, delivering and exchanging
offerings that have value for customers, clients, partners,
and society at large.

* Understanding customer needs :-
Example:-
Zomato studied that urban customers want convenience and
fast food delivery. It identified the need for quick service
and easy ordering

* Delivering customer value :-
Example:-
Netflix delivers value through :-

- Personalized recommendation
- ad-free entertainment
- Content anytime, anywhere

* Customer satisfaction:-
Example:-
If zomato delivers food before promised time then customer
feel delighted.
Nature of marketing:-

1. Customer oriented:-
Focus is on customer satisfaction rather than just profit.

2. Continuous process:-
Marketing is ongoing because customer preferences and
technology keep changing.

3. Integrated activity:-
Involves coordination of product, price, place and promotion.
(4 p's)

4. Value oriented:-
Marketing aims to creat superior value for customers.

5. Exchange based:-
Marketing is based on exchange money for goods/services.

| Basis    | Needs                   | Wants                                       |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Meaning  | Basic human requirement | desire for specific product to satisfy need |
| Nature   | Essential               | desire based                                |
| Creation | Natural                 | influenced by society & marketing           |
| Example  | Food, transportation    | Pizza, BMW car.                             |

* Influence on Needs:-

Marketing does not creat basic needs like hunger or shelter.
It identifies them and offer better solutions.
Example - Need → communication
Companies provide smartphones to satisfy that need.

* Influence on wants:-

Marketing strongly influences wants through advertising,
Branding packaging, celebrity endorsements, social
media marketing.

Example:-
A person needs clothing. Marketing influences them to want
branded clothes like premium fashion brands.

[Q_START: Q2 | Type: Analytical_Explanation | Weight: 20.0%]
Ques 2:- Explain marketing environment. Give its definition and characteristics
 with example.

Ans

Marketing environment refers to all forces around a
business that affect its marketing decisions.

Marketing environment consists of the actors and forces
outside and inside the organization that effect
management's ability to build and maintain
successful relationships with customers.

Characteristics of marketing environment:-

1. Dynamic in nature:-
The marketing environment is constantly changing,
technology changes, customer preference change, market
trends change.
Example:-
Growth of digital payments and online shopping changed
traditional retail businesses.

2. Uncertain:-
Future changes in the environment are difficult to
predict.
Example:-
During COVID-19 consumer buying behaviour suddenly
shifted towards online platform and essential goods.

3. Complex:-
Many factors affect business simultaneously and they
are interconnected.
Example:-
A change in government tax policy may increase product
price, reduce demand and affect profits at the same time.

4. Uncontrollable
External factors cannot be controlled by companies.
Example:-
Rise in fuel prices increases transportation costs which
affects product pricing.

Types of marketing environment:-

* Internal environment:-
Factors within the organization such as:
top management, company culture, financial resources

Example:-
If management focuses on innovation, the company will
invest more in research and development.

* External environment:-

1. Micro-environment:-
Factors close to the company.
• customers
• suppliers
• competitors
• marketing intermediaries

Example:-
If suppliers increase raw material prices, the company's
production cost increases.

2. Macro-environment:-
Large external forces affecting the entire industry.
• Demographic factors (age, income, population)
• Economic factors (inflation, income levels)
• Political & legal factors (laws)
• Technological factors (new innovation)

Ques 3:- Explain micro and macro external environment factors.

Ans Micro Environment:-

Includes forces that are close to the company and directly
affect its ability to serve customers

Factors:-

1. Customers
Types of customers:

1. Individual customers
2. Business buyers
3. Government buyers

Example:-
If customers shift preference from petrol cars to electric
vehicle, automobile companies must adapt.

2. Suppliers

Impact:- Delay in supply affects production, increase in
cost increase product price.

Example:-
Shortage of computer chips affected automobile production
globally.

3. Competitors:-

Types:-
(i) Brand competition
(ii) Product competition
(iii) Generic competition

Example
Coca cola and pepsico compete in the soft drink market.

Macro environment

Consist of large, broad forces that affect all industries
and businesses.

Factors:-

1. Demographic environment:-
Age, gender, income, education, family size.

Example:-
A young population increase demand for smartphones,
and online education.

2. Economic environment:-
includes income levels, inflation, interest rates,
economic growth.
Example:-
High inflation reduces spending on luxury goods.

3. Political and legal environment:-
includes tax policies, consumer protection laws, labour
laws.
Example:-
Implementation of GST changed pricing and supply
chains in India.
